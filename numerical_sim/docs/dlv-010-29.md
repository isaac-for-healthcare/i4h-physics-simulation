## Added acceptance coverage

`tests/runtime/test_direct_acceptance.py` parameterizes the exact registered IDs
over 1/8 environments and eager/CUDA-graph stepping. It preserves the authored
cases, action space, observations, numerical parameters and episode deadlines.

- Chole: an existing sphere-position servo drives the grasper to the gallbladder;
  contact pairs and applied tissue impulses prove contact, displacement is
  measured, and the grasper withdraws. Three complete episodes use normal
  timeout termination.
- Aorta: separate insertion, rotation and tip-bend commands change their control
  targets and physical state. Three complete episodes use normal timeouts.
- ReachTarget: the existing scripted straight insertion runs for 60 physics
  ticks, then holds. Three episodes must reach the authored target, earn the
  completion reward, terminate successfully and reset automatically.
- All tasks check finite actions, physical state, observations and rewards;
  declared observation shapes; selected-world restoration and byte-identical
  peer positions, histories, velocities, flags, rod orientations, control arrays
  and observed state products; close/reopen; and a
  checked PPO rollout/update with finite losses and changed policy parameters.
  Physical-state checks include the private fluid-pool block, not only the main
  tissue/rod block, including after the PPO update.

Each isolated matrix cell exits through normal pytest process shutdown. The
runner treats skips, collection errors and abnormal exits as failures. Videos
use an independent tiled camera on the installed candidate, keeping the task's
default observation layout. They show the production anatomy with the camera's
documented rendering limitations. No policy convergence is claimed.
